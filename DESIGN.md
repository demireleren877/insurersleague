# Design system
Deep navy #0B1020, graphite #151D30, white #F4F7FB, electric blue #43C6FF, amber #FFBE55. Light slate decision canvas with white surfaces, navy navigation. System sans for Turkish support, tabular numbers. Oversize condensed-looking heavy race numbers; restrained regular interface text. Team identities: Atlas cyan triangle, Pusula violet compass, Nova amber star, Kalkan mint shield, Mavi blue waves, Zirve coral mountains. Signature: long, identity-preserving race lanes, with a crisp vertical finish grid. Four-step strategy workflow on a light center surface, sticky dark live policy card to the right. Rounded 8–14px controls and cards, thin cool borders, generous spacing. Stage: dark, big numbers, 750ms lane translation. Reduced motion: instant transitions.
